NETLIFY DEPLOYMENT STEPS
========================

1. Open your web browser and go to https://app.netlify.com/

2. Click on "Sign up" if you don't have an account, or "Sign in" if you do

3. Choose your preferred login method:
   - GitHub
   - GitLab
   - Bitbucket
   - Email

4. After logging in, go to https://app.netlify.com/drop

5. Drag and drop the deployment-package.zip file from:
   C:\Users\futte\Desktop\we are close\deployment-package.zip
   onto the drop zone on the Netlify page

6. Wait for the deployment to complete (usually takes 1-2 minutes)

7. Netlify will provide you with a unique URL for your site that looks like:
   https://your-site-name.netlify.app

8. You can customize this URL by clicking on "Site settings" then "Change site name"

Your deployment package contains:
- index.html (main page)
- assets folder with CSS, JavaScript, and media files

The site will be fully functional once deployed!
